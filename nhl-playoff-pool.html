How It Works

  1. Start a pool. Choose the Playoffs pool and you get a 6-digit pool code.
  2. Share the code. Text it, post it in the group chat or pin it in the office Slack.
  3. Everyone fills in the entry form. Pick 12 players and 5 teams (see below). Use initials or a nickname — don't use your real name.
  4. Watch the leaderboard. Scores update automatically all through the playoffs. Click any entry to see their picks once entries close.

Scoring

  1. 1 point for every playoff point (goal or assist) scored by your 10 skaters.
  2. 1 point for every playoff win by your 5 teams.
  3. 1 point for every 20 saves by your 2 goaltenders. Each save counts, so 30 saves = 1.5 points.

Skater points are goals plus assists. A goalie who makes 30 saves in a game earns 1.5 points, so a hot goalie on a long run matters about as much as a top forward. Every team win is worth a point, which rewards picking clubs that go deep.

When Entries Open and Close

Entries open as soon as all 16 playoff teams are known, and close automatically when the first playoff game starts. Picks stay hidden until then, so nobody can copy the leader. You can start a pool any time and share the code early.

Tips for Winning a Playoff Pool

  • Lean on players from teams you expect to play four rounds — more games means more points.
  • Goalies on deep-running teams pile up saves; check the Playoff Bracket and Stanley Cup odds before you pick.
  • Defencemen who run the power play score far more than the rest.
  • Don't stack one team — one early upset can sink an entry.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the NHL playoff pool really free?

Yes. There is no cost, no sign-up and no email address needed. Hockey Pooler does not handle money; any prizes are up to your group.

How does scoring work?

You get 1 point for every playoff point (goal or assist) scored by your 10 skaters, 1 point for every playoff win by your 5 teams, and 1 point for every 20 saves by your 2 goalies (each save counts, so 30 saves = 1.5 points). Highest total wins.

When do entries close?

Entries open once all 16 playoff teams are known and close automatically when the first playoff game starts. Picks stay hidden until entries close.

Can I change my picks after entering?

No. Once an entry is submitted it is final, just like a paper pool.

How many people can join one pool?

As many as you like: a family, a group chat or a whole office can share one pool code.
